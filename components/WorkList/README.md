# WorkList

Hairline rows: title (and an optional short `note`) on the left, the year in mono on the right. Hovering a row dims its siblings to `ink-muted` and reveals a `↗`; this is the system's signature hover. Provide `items` as `{title, year, href, note}`.

- Newest first. Three to six items; link the rest from a "All work →" text button.
- Notes are two or three words in lowercase: "message routing", "internship".
- Don't add thumbnails or descriptions to the rows; put those on the case-study page.
